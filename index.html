02 / Research

Research paper · IEEE TPS 2023

Efficiently Supporting ABAC in Relational Databases

Aug 2022 – May 2023

Developed an ABAC framework for MySQL with policy-tree pruning and context-aware rules for efficient, fine-grained authorization.

Read research highlights
  • Architected and implemented a comprehensive Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC) framework for MySQL, extending fine-grained, dynamic control across all database objects including tables, views, stored procedures, functions, and triggers.
  • Optimized the ABAC framework by integrating Poltree, a policy-tree data structure, which intelligently pruned policies, leading to a substantial decrease in access grant latency by improving the time complexity of policy retrieval from O(N) to O(A), particularly under high-load conditions with a large number of complex rules.
  • Extended the authorization model to support environmental attributes such as time, location, and IP address, enabling context-aware, adaptive security policies beyond traditional access control systems.
